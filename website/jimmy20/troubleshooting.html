Troubleshooting

Jimmy Next runs its bundled engine (based on Nexus — see Credits) rather than connecting to a separate program, so most first-run issues come down to that engine starting up, or your radio connection, rather than a WSJT-X link.

Jimmy Next seems to hang or freeze right after opening

The very first time Jimmy Next launches its bundled engine, Windows Defender (or another antivirus) may take several seconds to scan the new, unsigned executable before it's allowed to run — the installer runs it once during setup specifically to get this scan out of the way early, but a slow security product can still cause a brief pause on first real use. This only happens once; if it happens on every launch, or lasts more than a few seconds, that's worth reporting (see below).

No audio or radio control

Open Options > Radio and confirm your radio and audio devices are selected there — Jimmy Next needs this configured to transmit and to key your radio, since it isn't relying on a separate program's settings. See Settings for what's on that tab.

Backing up your data

Jimmy Next keeps your settings, logbook, and rule definitions in one folder: %LocalAppData%\Jimmy Next\ (type that path into File Explorer's address bar, or press Win+R and paste it there). Close Jimmy Next first, then copy that whole folder somewhere safe — your logbook database, settings file, and any saved profiles all live inside it. To restore, close Jimmy Next and copy the backed-up folder back into place.

Getting help

Open an issue on GitHub to ask questions or report problems. When asking about a problem, please mention that you're running Jimmy Next (not production Jimmy) and include your version number, shown in the title bar or via Ctrl+S — the two share history but not code, so version and product matter for anyone trying to help.

Create Support Report

If something's not working and you need to describe it to someone else, open the Help dialog and choose Create Support Report. It walks you through a short form — your callsign, name, and email are optional, but a description of the problem is required — and then asks where to save the ZIP file (Downloads is offered). The ZIP contains:

  • What you typed in the form.
  • Jimmy Next's version, Windows version, and current connection status.
  • Your Jimmy Next folder: settings (with passwords and logins automatically removed), log files, and the logbook move reports. Downloaded lookup data is left out.
  • Your logbook, if Include my logbook is checked (it is unless you uncheck it).

Under How to send it, choose Send to KB0UZT (the default) or Save to my computer only. Send to KB0UZT copies the report's location and opens a private upload page: choose Add files, press Control+V to paste, press Enter, then Upload. Only KB0UZT can see what is uploaded. Nothing is sent until you upload it.

Reporting a bug

Jimmy Next is public beta software — please report anything that looks wrong, even something small. A Support Report sent with Send to KB0UZT (see above) is the most useful format. Please don't attach a Support Report to a GitHub issue — issues are public, and the report can hold your logbook.
