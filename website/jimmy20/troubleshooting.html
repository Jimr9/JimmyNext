Troubleshooting

Jimmy Next runs its bundled engine (based on Nexus — see Credits) rather than connecting to a separate program, so most first-run issues come down to that engine starting up, or your radio connection, rather than a WSJT-X link.

Jimmy Next seems to hang or freeze right after opening

The very first time Jimmy Next launches its bundled engine, Windows Defender (or another antivirus) may take several seconds to scan the new, unsigned executable before it's allowed to run — the installer runs it once during setup specifically to get this scan out of the way early, but a slow security product can still cause a brief pause on first real use. This only happens once; if it happens on every launch, or lasts more than a few seconds, that's worth reporting (see below).

No audio or radio control

Open Options > Radio and confirm your radio and audio devices are selected there — Jimmy Next needs this configured to transmit and to key your radio, since it isn't relying on a separate program's settings. See Settings for what's on that tab.

Getting help

The tilly-beta discussion group is the best place to ask questions or see what other operators are running into. When asking about a problem, please mention that you're running Jimmy Next (not production Jimmy) and include your version number, shown in the title bar or via Ctrl+S — the two share history but not code, so version and product matter for anyone trying to help.

Create Support Report

If something's not working and you need to describe it to someone else, open the Help dialog and choose Create Support Report. It walks you through a short form — your callsign, name, and email are optional, but a description of the problem is required — and then builds a ZIP file in your Downloads folder containing:

  • What you typed in the form.
  • Jimmy Next's version, Windows version, and current connection status.
  • Your Jimmy Next settings, with passwords and API keys automatically stripped out.
  • Recent log files and recent decode history.

Nothing is sent automatically — Jimmy Next only creates the ZIP so you can review it and send it yourself.

Reporting a bug

Jimmy Next is public beta software — please report anything that looks wrong, even something small. The discussion group works for quick questions; for a specific reproducible bug, opening an issue on GitHub with a Support Report attached (see above) is the most useful format.
